# v0.1.4 — Complete Management UI Blueprint

## Summary

This release transforms the limited executive preview into a complete,
management-presentable HSF ERP user-interface blueprint while intentionally
adding no production business functionality.

## UI coverage

- One executive dashboard
- Nineteen connected management domains
- Four hundred planned module screens
- Generic UI-only new-record, edit, and record-detail previews for applicable
  routes
- Responsive desktop, tablet, and mobile navigation
- Management dashboards, registries, workflows, forms, reports, calendars,
  document libraries, profiles, settings, approval views, and audit-history
  patterns

## Included domains

- Planning and staff performance
- Projects and locations
- Field requisitions and MFR
- Procurement
- Finance and accounting
- HR and recruitment
- Payroll
- E4BL education
- A2PHC health
- Climate Action
- Women Empowerment
- Inventory and assets
- Donors, CSR, grants, and sponsorship
- MEAL and impact
- Meetings, resolutions, notices, and communication
- Training and learning materials
- Documents and evidence
- Reports and analytics
- Administration and system configuration

## Product controls preserved

- Every screen is marked as a UI blueprint.
- All displayed values and people are synthetic demonstration content.
- Route availability does not claim authentication, authorization, persistence,
  calculation, approval, posting, clinical, payroll, notification, upload,
  export, or integration functionality.
- Existing HSF product truths, financial invariants, clinical boundaries,
  privacy rules, and open decisions remain unchanged.
