## Product clarifications captured

- Teachers are assigned to E4BL.
- Head Teachers review reporting and submit next-month requisitions.
- Health Supervisors are assigned to A2PHC and submit daily patient and camp
  data.
- Doctors are assigned to A2PHC and should complete the clinical section of the
  same patient visit.
- The final Doctor dataset remains an open HSF decision.
- Daily entries can generate a reviewed monthly achievement draft.
- Field requisitions are consolidated by Project Coordinators into MFRs.
- Field Requisition and MFR remain separate controlled documents.
- Mixed measurement units must not be added into misleading totals.
