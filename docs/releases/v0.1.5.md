# HSF ERP v0.1.5
## A2PHC UI Expansion and Role-Aware Module Visibility

**Release type:** UI feature patch  
**Baseline:** `v0.1.4` complete responsive management UI blueprint  
**Functional status:** Presentation only; no real business persistence or authorization

## Highlights

- Added a complete, dedicated A2PHC Field Operations UI.
- Merged health-camp planning, team assignment, patients, visits, Doctor consultation, prescription, referral, follow-up, equipment, travel, field expense, requisition, MFR traceability, achievement, reporting, privacy, and future medicine planning into one A2PHC experience.
- Added mobile-first workspaces for Medical Supervisors and General Physicians.
- Added a global role-visibility preview for module and route navigation.
- Kept E4BL and A2PHC workspaces separated for project-specific users.
- Preserved all non-A2PHC UI pages and routes.

## A2PHC scope added

### Field operations

- five-district coverage presentation;
- mobile team and assignment views;
- camp plan, calendar, route, travel, and readiness;
- motorcycle, local transport, and boat-access planning.

### Patient and clinical UI

- Patient Master and Patient Visit separation;
- patient registration and queue;
- approved basic measurements;
- Doctor consultation workspace;
- diagnosis, prescription, test advice, referral, follow-up, high-risk flag, and clinical sign-off.

### Equipment and finance UI

- medical equipment custody and condition;
- maintenance and calibration;
- fuel, transport, boat fare, communication, printing, and field expenses;
- bills, evidence, advances, and adjustment;
- Supervisor requisition → Coordinator consolidation → MFR → approval → disbursement traceability.

### Reporting UI

- daily camp summary;
- Doctor and Supervisor achievement;
- monthly project management report;
- project achievement report;
- coverage and reach;
- complaint and diagnosis trends;
- data-quality review.

## Role-aware visibility

The UI preview now supports role-specific module visibility. Examples:

- E4BL users do not see A2PHC workspaces.
- A2PHC users do not see E4BL workspaces.
- General Physicians see assigned clinical options rather than field finance or education.
- Medical Supervisors see camp, patient, equipment, expense, and reporting options.
- Executive users retain organization-wide visibility.
- System Administrators do not automatically receive business approval authority.

## Medicine rule

Regular medicine dispensing remains visibly disabled. The UI includes future medicine-support planning only. Activation requires approved budget, procurement, stock, expiry, issue, reconciliation, and audit rules.

## Security boundary

This release demonstrates visibility only. It does not replace:

- secure authentication;
- server-side authorization;
- protected routes;
- project and location scope;
- ownership rules;
- patient privacy policy;
- database row-level security;
- audit persistence.

## Compatibility

The patch is additive and targets the `v0.1.4` complete UI blueprint. It adds a dedicated A2PHC route surface and one global UI-only role preview layer without redesigning other modules.
