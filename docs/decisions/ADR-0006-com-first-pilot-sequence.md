# ADR-0006: COM-First Implementation Sequence

**Status:** Management direction recorded; implementation plan pending normal gates.  
**Decision date:** 30 October 2026  
**Decision owner:** HSF management (explicit instruction in the current project conversation)

## Decision

After platform access and Admin foundation: pilot **Head Office/COM first**, **E4BL Hazaribagh second**, **A2PHC Bogura third**, then expand to accepted current areas, Climate Action & Resilience and future approved programmes. This supersedes A2PHC-first staging in older planning documents **for future implementation planning only**. It does not change current approved programme status or grant any business authority.

## Rationale

An initial Head Office pilot provides a bounded environment to validate identity, scoped permissions, administration, separation of duties, audit and one minimum institutional workflow before introducing protected learner records and then higher-sensitivity clinical records.

## Constraints

COM is a central organizational unit, not automatically a public programme. The first COM transaction shall remain a **proposed minimum requisition workflow** until management approves actual forms, actors and limits. No automatic approval of new policy, budgets, role names, funding thresholds, clinical fields or programme activation is implied. Historical PRD v1.0.0 retains its proposed-baseline status; update by formal document change control rather than overwriting.

## Consequences

The next phase is G1 individual identity and a read-only COM authorization slice, followed by Admin Control Centre capability and separately accepted COM workflow. E4BL and A2PHC stay blueprint/disabled until their specific gates. Ensure governance/Knowledge Hub terminology remains coordinated without silently rewriting its mirror.
