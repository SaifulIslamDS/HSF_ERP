# A2PHC Open Product and Management Decisions

## Decision rule

Codex must not invent policy for clinical safety, patient privacy, financial
authority, or staff responsibility.

Where a decision is not required for a reversible technical foundation, keep the
behavior configurable and mark it provisional.

## A. Programme and geography

- Confirm current active upazilas and unions in all five districts.
- Confirm current camp frequency by team.
- Confirm whether teams are permanently district-based or rotate.
- Select the first pilot district and unions.

## B. Supervisor workflow

- Final mandatory patient-registration fields.
- Final approved basic measurements.
- Who may correct registration after Doctor consultation starts?
- Who is responsible for referral follow-up?
- Required daily camp summary fields.

## C. Doctor workflow

- Mandatory clinical fields.
- Diagnosis coding method.
- Prescription format.
- Test-advice format.
- Referral categories and destinations.
- High-risk criteria.
- Clinical sign-off method.
- Correction and amendment policy after sign-off.

## D. Privacy and consent

- Patient-consent method.
- Minor/guardian consent.
- Medical-record retention.
- Export authority.
- Whether patient mobile number is mandatory, optional, or unavailable.
- Who may see full patient identity?
- Who may see clinical detail?
- Lost-device procedure.

## E. Field finance

- Fuel-calculation rule.
- Motorcycle-use and maintenance rule.
- Boat-fare evidence.
- Local transport evidence.
- Daily allowance or refreshment policy.
- Advance/IOU settlement period.
- Expense-approval authority.
- Requisition deadline.
- MFR approval flow.

## F. Equipment

- Final equipment list.
- Custodian.
- Calibration responsibility.
- Repair approval.
- Loss and damage process.
- Replacement authority.

## G. Medicine

Current rule:

```text
Regular medicine dispensing is disabled.
```

Before future activation, approve:

- funding source;
- medicine list;
- procurement authority;
- storage;
- batch and expiry;
- issue responsibility;
- prescription link;
- reconciliation;
- disposal;
- audit.
