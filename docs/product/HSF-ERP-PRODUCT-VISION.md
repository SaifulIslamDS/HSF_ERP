# 1. Introduction

ERP, or **Enterprise Resource Planning software**, is often described as an organization’s **“single source of truth.”**

This means that reliable information about departments, people, projects, money, documents, decisions, approvals, achievements, and reports is connected through one controlled system.

Many ready-to-use ERP products are available in the market, but no single generic product serves every purpose of every organization. A garment manufacturer, hospital, educational institution, NGO, and foundation have different operating models. Even two organizations in the same sector may use different approval structures, reporting formats, funding controls, responsibilities, and decision-making processes.

For this reason, many institutions design customized systems around their own work, pace, nature, culture, and long-term goals. A customized ERP does more than digitize existing work. It reorganizes, connects, controls, and prepares the institution for sustainable growth.

For a long time, I have wanted to apply my programming knowledge and software-development skills to build such a unified system for Human Safety Foundation. During the last several months, the need for this platform has become increasingly clear.

HSF now operates across education, primary healthcare, climate action, women empowerment, child sponsorship, central-office management, finance, payroll, recruitment, donor management, project reporting, and field operations.

Important parts of these operations are currently managed through Excel, Google Forms, email, WhatsApp, bank statements, paper bills, vouchers, and individual follow-up. These methods support the current work, but they also create increasing risks as the organization grows:

- repeated data entry
- disconnected information
- inconsistent versions
- unclear approval history
- delayed reporting
- limited real-time visibility
- dependence on individual spreadsheet knowledge

HSF ERP is being designed to address these challenges.

Alhamdulillah, the initial interface has already been developed. It is not yet a complete ERP. It is the professional foundation, system architecture, brand identity, and first visible step of a long-term software journey.

# 2. What HSF ERP Is

HSF ERP is a customized NGO Enterprise Resource Planning and Management Information System for Human Safety Foundation.

It will connect the organization’s complete operational lifecycle through one digital platform.

```text
Annual Planning
    ↓
Annual Budget
    ↓
Monthly Plan and Requisition
    ↓
MFR and Approval
    ↓
Fund Disbursement and Procurement
    ↓
Field Implementation
    ↓
Daily and Monthly Achievement Reporting
    ↓
Bills, Vouchers, and Evidence
    ↓
Financial and Project Reporting
    ↓
Monthly Closing
    ↓
Donor and Annual Reporting
```

In practical terms:

> A plan, its approval, funding, implementation, daily work, achievement, expenditure, evidence, result, and report will remain connected through one reliable digital chain.

# 3. Why HSF Needs a Customized ERP

Ready-made systems usually focus on one limited area:

- accounting
- HR
- school management
- hospital management
- donor management

HSF is simultaneously:

- an NGO
- an education service provider
- a primary healthcare provider
- a donor-funded organization
- a field-operation institution
- a human-resource entity
- a project-based organization
- a community-development institution

Therefore, HSF does not only need accounting software, school software, hospital software, or HR software.

It needs an integrated platform where finance, HR, education, health, project management, procurement, donor reporting, daily activity, and annual reporting are connected.

# 4. Core Principles

## 4.1 One Organization, One System

All HSF projects, departments, users, locations, donors, funds, banks, students, graduates, patients, staff activities, requisitions, and reports will exist within one system.

## 4.2 One Entry, Multiple Uses

Information should be entered once and reused in relevant workflows, dashboards, and reports.

A health supervisor’s patient entry can contribute to:

- daily patient register
- camp report
- district report
- disease summary
- demographic report
- doctor-wise summary
- monthly health report
- donor report
- annual impact report

A teacher’s daily attendance and activity entry can contribute to:

- daily attendance
- monthly achievement report
- class performance
- teacher activity report
- student-risk alert
- parent-meeting report
- annual education report

## 4.3 Plan Before Spending

Every expenditure should be connected to an annual plan, budget, field requisition, MFR, and approval.

## 4.4 Every Transaction and Achievement Must Be Traceable

The system should show:

- who entered the record
- which project and location it belongs to
- which plan or activity it supports
- the approved target
- the actual achievement
- available evidence
- reviewer and approval history
- related budget and MFR

## 4.5 Accountability by Design

Submission, review, return, correction, approval, rejection, and closure will maintain a complete digital history.

# 6. Authentication, Roles, and Project Assignment

Every user will have:

- secure login
- user profile

- assigned project
- assigned location
- permissions
- reporting supervisor
- activity history

## 6.1 Project-based User Groups

Users will not only belong to HSF as an organization. They will also be assigned to specific project groups.

### E4BL Group

- Project Coordinator
- Head Teacher
- Teacher
- Helping Hand
- Education Data Reviewer
- Authorized Finance User

### A2PHC Group

- Project Coordinator
- Medical Camp Supervisor
- General Physician
- Health Data Reviewer
- Medicine or Inventory User
- Authorized Finance User

This means:

- Teachers will see and submit only relevant E4BL centre and class information.
- Health supervisors will submit information only for assigned camps, service areas, or districts.
- Doctors will access assigned A2PHC camps and provide clinical input.
- Project coordinators will review all submissions within their own project.
- Senior management will access cross-project dashboards.

## 6.2 Segregation of Duties

- A requester cannot provide final approval for the same request.
- A supervisor may register patients, but the doctor provides clinical sign-off.
- A project coordinator consolidates field requisitions but does not provide final financial approval.
- Finance reviews budget and coding but does not alter project achievements.

# 8. Project Management and Staff Achievement Reporting

A major part of the Project Management module will be staff work planning and achievement reporting.

Depending on the role, each staff member will:

- submit daily activity records each day; or
- submit a month-end achievement report where daily entry is not appropriate.

Reporting frequency will be configurable by role.

## 8.1 Purpose of Daily Entry

Daily entry is suitable when work:

- occurs every day
- is directly related to service delivery
- must later be consolidated into a monthly report
- is class-, attendance-, camp-, or visit-based
- loses data quality when entered late

## 8.2 Monthly Achievement Report

The monthly report may contain:

- Staff ID
- Name
- Designation
- Project
- Location
- Reporting Month
- Planned Activity
- Indicator
- Unit
- Monthly Target
- Monthly Achievement
- Achievement Percentage
- Evidence
- Challenge
- Corrective Action
- Next-month Priority
- Supervisor Comment
- Review Status

## 8.3 Automatic Monthly Consolidation

Where daily entries are required, the system will generate a monthly draft automatically.

```text
Daily Class and Attendance Entries
    ↓
Teacher Monthly Achievement Draft
    ↓
Teacher Review and Submission
    ↓
Head Teacher Verification
    ↓
Project Coordinator Approval
```

```text
Daily Camp and Patient Entries
    ↓
Supervisor Monthly Achievement Draft
    ↓
Supervisor Review and Submission
    ↓
Project Coordinator / Health Reviewer Verification
```

## 8.4 Mixed-unit Control

Students, days, sessions, meetings, reports, patients, and materials are different units.

The system will not add these into one misleading total. Achievement percentages will be calculated separately by indicator. Any weighted score must use management-approved weights.

# 11. Recommended Doctor Reporting Model

The exact doctor dataset is not yet finalized. However, doctors should not be required to re-enter the patient list, because that would create duplicate work and inconsistent records.

The recommended model is:

> The supervisor records patient-registration and operational information, while the doctor completes and signs off the clinical section of the same visit.

## 11.1 Patient-level Doctor Input

Doctors may enter or confirm:

- Clinical Complaint Review
- Provisional or Confirmed Diagnosis
- Prescription
- Recommended Medicine
- Test Advice
- Referral Decision
- Follow-up Required
- Follow-up Timeframe
- High-risk Case Flag
- Clinical Notes
- Consultation Completion
- Digital Sign-off

## 11.2 Daily Doctor Camp Summary

At the end of a camp, a doctor may provide:

- Camp Date and Location
- Total Consultations — system-generated
- Common Disease Pattern
- Serious or High-risk Cases
- Referral Count
- Follow-up Required Count
- Medicine Shortage or Clinical Supply Need
- Public Health Observation
- Maternal, Child, or Elderly Health Concern
- Clinical Quality Issue
- Recommendation for the Next Camp

## 11.3 Rules to Avoid Duplicate Data Entry

- The supervisor enters name, contact, and demographic details.
- The doctor opens the same visit and completes the clinical section.
- Patient counts are calculated from visit records.
- Doctors enter clinical data and provide sign-off.
- Voice-to-text clinical notes may be introduced later.
- Incomplete clinical records appear as pending.

## 11.4 Outputs

This model can produce:

- Doctor-wise Consultation Report
- Disease Trend Report
- Referral Report
- Follow-up Report
- High-risk Case Register
- Clinical Quality Dashboard
- Medicine Need Forecast
- Monthly A2PHC Medical Summary

## 12.4 Requisition to MFR

```text
Head Teacher / Medical Supervisor Submits Requisition
    ↓
Project Coordinator Reviews
    ↓
Duplicate, Stock, and Budget Check
    ↓
Coordinator Consolidates Field Requisitions
    ↓
Project-level Monthly Plan Finalized
    ↓
Project Coordinator Prepares MFR
    ↓
Finance Reviews Budget and Account Coding
    ↓
Management Recommendation
    ↓
Final Approval
```

## 12.5 Important Controls

- A field requisition is not an MFR.
- The field user identifies operational need.
- The project coordinator verifies need, plan, budget, and priority before preparing the MFR.
- Reductions, exclusions, or changes require justification.
- Requisition lines should remain traceable to MFR lines.
- Adding an unapproved need requires a reason.
- The monthly cut-off date should be configurable until management formally approves a deadline.

# 30. Conclusion

HSF ERP is not merely a software-development project.

It is an institutional initiative to make Human Safety Foundation more organized, transparent, efficient, accountable, and future-ready.

The system will connect more than finance and reporting:

- Annual plans will connect with daily work.
- Field staff achievements will become visible.
- Teacher activity will connect with E4BL results.
- Health-supervisor patient data will form the foundation of A2PHC reporting.
- Doctor clinical input will become part of the same patient visit.
- Head Teacher and Medical Supervisor next-month requisitions will form the basis of the Project Coordinator’s MFR.
- MFRs will connect to expenditure, and expenditure will connect to results and evidence.

The most important change will be this:

> HSF will no longer depend on individuals, isolated Excel files, or disconnected communication channels for institutional truth. Reliable information about plans, funds, people, daily work, achievements, evidence, and results will remain within HSF’s own controlled system.

Alhamdulillah, the first visible step of this journey has already been completed.

This is still the beginning, but it is a strong, structured, and long-term beginning.
